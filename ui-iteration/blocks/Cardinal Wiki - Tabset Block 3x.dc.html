Turn 1  block-tabs
The tabset block, in both aesthetics
Today blocks/block-tabs/component.js draws its own card: 6px radius, drop shadow, a white-to-grey gradient strip, Quasar blue for the active tab. None of that belongs to either aesthetic. The block keeps its structure (strip of role=tab buttons over one role=tabpanel, slotted light-DOM panels, arrow-key walk, reveal-on-anchor) and takes its look from the page through the --tabs-* custom properties on :host. Each frame is the block sitting in the article column, light and dark.
1a
Ledger
A hairline frame with two corner marks. Tinted strip, white active tab joined to the panel by a 2px accent inset.

From source

The image ships with PostgreSQL bindings and a read-only default config. Mount your own at /etc/cardinal.

docker run -d --name cardinal \ -p 3000:3000 -v cardinal-data:/data \ ghcr.io/cardinal-wiki/cardinal:3

Once the container reports healthy, open the setup wizard at port 3000.
