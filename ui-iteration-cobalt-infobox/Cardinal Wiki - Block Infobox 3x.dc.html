Montreal is the largest city in Quebec and the second most populous in Canada. It sits on an island in the Saint Lawrence River, with Mont Royal, the hill the city takes its name from, at its centre.

The metropolitan area runs to some four million people across the island and the south shore. French is the working language; most residents also speak English, and a third speak a third language at home. Winters are long and the city is built for them: the underground network links metro stations, offices and shopping over thirty kilometres.
