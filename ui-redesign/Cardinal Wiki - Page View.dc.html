Prerequisites

Before starting a rollout, confirm that the ingest queue depth is below 50k messages and that no backfill job is running. Deploys during a backfill will double-process the window.

Draining takes about four minutes per node. Budget 25 minutes for the full fleet.

Rolling deploy

Run the deploy from the release host. The cardinal-ctl wrapper handles node ordering and health gates for you.

cardinal-ctl deploy ingest-workers \ --revision "$GIT_SHA" \ --batch 2 --gate p99_latency<250ms
  • ▸Batches of two keep at least 80% of consumers alive.
  • ▸The gate aborts the rollout if p99 latency crosses 250 ms.
  • ▸Failed nodes are left drained, not restarted.
Rollback

Rollback is a forward deploy of the previous revision. There is no state to unwind — offsets are committed only after a successful flush.
